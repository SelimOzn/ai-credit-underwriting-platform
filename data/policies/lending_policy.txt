Rule 1:
Applicants with credit score below 580 must be rejected.

Rule 2:
Debt-to-income ratio above 0.80 requires manual review.

Rule 3:
Employment history below 1 year increases risk.

Rule 4:
Loan-to-income ratio above 8.0 requires supervisor approval.

Rule 5:
Applicants with score above 720 and low debt burden may be auto approved.
